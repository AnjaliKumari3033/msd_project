[01] Paradigm

Binary Sentiment Classification

Four distinct models of increasing capacity were trained and evaluated on 50,000 balanced reviews (25,000 train / 25,000 test). The performance demonstrates that expanding from lexical frequency to bidirectional contextual self-attention yields a 9.96% absolute accuracy increase.

Model Architecture Representation Accuracy Precision Recall F1-Score Architectural Evaluation
Multinomial Naive Bayes Bag-of-Words (Count) 84.20% 0.84 0.84 0.84 Probabilistic baseline; completely blind to word sequence, failing on negation phrases like “not good”.
Logistic Regression TF-IDF (Unigrams + Bigrams, $C=1.0$) 88.22% 0.88 0.88 0.88 Bigrams capture phrase-level negation weights, establishing an effective linear decision boundary.
Bidirectional LSTM Dense 128-dim Embeddings, 2-layer BiLSTM 87.78% 0.88 0.88 0.8778 Processes sequences sequentially; however, long document lengths (>200 tokens) cause temporal gradient saturation.
BERT Transformer (Fine-Tuned) WordPiece Tokens + Self-Attention 94.16% 0.94 0.94 0.94 State-of-the-art; global bidirectional self-attention captures long-range dependencies, sarcasm, and qualifiers.

[05] Deductions

Six Documented Linguistic & Dataset Anomalies

ANOMALY 01 Corpus Architecture

The Bimodal Polarizing “J-Curve” and Missing Neutral Ratings

The continuous 1–10 rating distribution has an intentional gap: ratings 5 and 6 are almost entirely absent. The creators of the benchmark IMDB dataset intentionally discarded neutral reviews to enforce strict binary polarity. In real-world data collection, reviewers rarely write when indifferent; submissions concentrate heavily at the extremes (1/10 and 10/10).

Impact: Regression models face harsh penalties for intermediate predictions, forcing learning algorithms toward extreme boundary transitions.
ANOMALY 02 Linguistic Asymmetry

The Punctuation and Capitalization “Anger Index”

Regression analysis revealed that negative sentiment is expressed far more aggressively than positive praise. 1-star reviews contain on average 3.8x more exclamation marks (!!!), consecutive question marks, and ALL-CAPS shouting than 10-star reviews. Positive reviewers rely on sophisticated adjectives; dissatisfied reviewers rely on orthographic emphasis.

Impact: Retaining casing and punctuation in subword tokenization gave BERT a powerful secondary predictive feature that bag-of-words lowercasing discarded.
ANOMALY 03 Reviewer Psychology

The U-Shaped Document Length Paradox

Review length exhibits a pronounced U-shaped curve when plotted against user score. 1-star and 10-star reviews average over 1,420 characters, while moderate reviews average under 950 characters. Intense emotional reaction motivates reviewers to draft exhaustive narrative justifications, whereas mild opinions generate brief summaries.

Impact: Sequential networks (BiLSTM) experienced gradient decay across these lengthy reviews, whereas Transformer self-attention processed all tokens in parallel.
ANOMALY 04 Matrix Mathematics

The 99.2% TF-IDF Matrix Sparsity Barrier

Extracting unigrams and bigrams across 50,000 documents produced a vocabulary matrix exceeding 80,000 unique terms, with over 99.2% zero entries. Universal terms like “film” and “character” contributed zero discriminative weight despite massive frequency counts.

Impact: Truncated SVD successfully compressed this 80,000-dimensional space down to 100 components, preserving 85% of latent variance and enabling fast clustering.
ANOMALY 05 Model Architecture

Recurrent Gradient Decay on Extended Documents

The 2-layer Bidirectional LSTM achieved 87.78% accuracy, which was slightly below regularized Logistic Regression with bigrams (88.22%). Because movie reviews average 200–300 tokens, recurrent memory cells struggle to propagate temporal backpropagation over hundreds of steps without information loss.

Impact: BERT eliminated this bottleneck through constant-time pairwise attention between all word positions, leaping to 94.16% accuracy.
ANOMALY 06 Unsupervised Ambiguity

Hard Partitioning Collapse in Mixed Reviews

K-Means hard cluster assignments struggled on reviews exhibiting mixed sentiment (e.g. “Superb acting, but an incoherent mess of a plot”). Forcing an absolute discrete cluster label distorted topical coherence. Gaussian Mixture Models (GMM) resolved this by calculating posterior probabilities (e.g. 60% positive, 40% negative).

Impact: Confirmed that probabilistic soft-clustering is mathematically superior for real-world unlabelled opinion mining.
