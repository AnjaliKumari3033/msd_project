BERT Transformer

State-of-the-Art Deep Learning

Architecture & Fine-Tuning

Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers (BERT) represents the pinnacle of modern NLP performance. Rather than training from scratch, we leveraged Transfer Learning by fine-tuning Google's bert-base-uncased model.

Pipeline specifics:

  • Utilizing the pre-trained WordPiece Tokenizer.
  • Leveraging massive pre-trained contextual embeddings learned from reading Wikipedia and the BookCorpus.
  • Fine-tuning the final classification head over 3 epochs on a GPU.

Results

The fine-tuned BERT model achieved an outstanding 94.16% accuracy and a 0.9416 Macro F1-Score on the test set, utterly dominating the traditional machine learning approaches. By employing Self-Attention mechanisms, BERT is able to perfectly understand negation, sarcasm, and long-range dependencies within the movie reviews.
